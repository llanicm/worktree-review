# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't accepted in issues, pull requests
or anywhere else in the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@llanicm](https://github.com/llanicm), privately.
Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block contributors
who don't follow this code.
